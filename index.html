Building at the boundary
of analog and digital

I'm an ECE graduate of San Diego State University with a focus on analog/mixed-signal IC design, RF, and power electronics. My work spans from designing biomedical systems at the transistor level in 45nm CMOS to building switching flyback power supplies for industry. I've worked across the full stack of analog and mixed-signal hardware.

My main project was designing a switching flyback power supply for General Atomics, engineering a custom transformer from core selection through PCB, leading hardware design reviews, and coordinating directly with component vendors. Alongside that, I've built a 12-bit SAR ADC from scratch in 45nm CMOS and run an independent wireless charging desk project applying RF near-field principles.

I'm fluent in English, Russian, and Georgian, and I bring the same methodical, hands-on approach to hardware that I bring to everything I build.

Flyback Power Supply — General Atomics
Power Electronics Industry Featured

Designed a switching flyback power supply for a linear actuator load in collaboration with General Atomics. Engineered a custom flyback transformer and led all hardware design reviews as student hardware lead.

  • Engineered a custom flyback transformer: calculated turns ratio, core selection, and parasitic modeling to optimize power transfer efficiency
  • Performed full KiCad schematic simulation to validate performance before PCB fabrication
  • Led hardware design reviews and served as primary point of contact with component vendors, evaluating datasheets and making final component selections
  • Characterized switching transients and leakage inductance via oscilloscope, observing ~1.7 kV peak spikes at ~80.6 kHz switching frequency

Mixed-Signal Glucose Meter IC
Mixed-Signal Capstone Lead

Led ADC/DAC design for a fully integrated glucose monitoring system-on-chip in 45nm CMOS. Covered the full flow from MATLAB biosensor modeling and Verilog RTL to full custom Cadence Virtuoso implementation.

  • Developed clean and noisy biosensor signal models in MATLAB to define ADC input specs: dynamic range, noise floor, and bandwidth
  • Implemented RTL models of the full signal chain in Verilog, covering DAC bias voltage generation (VBIAS) and ADC front-end, for digital integration verification before Cadence implementation
  • Designed full custom ADC including 12-bit SAR logic, dynamic comparator, bootstrapped S/H, and CMOS output buffer at transistor level in Cadence Virtuoso (gpdk045)
  • System integrates temperature compensation, EEPROM calibration, and LCD output, covering the full mixed-signal SoC design flow

Wireless Charging Desk
RF / Wireless Independent

Designing a multi-device wireless charging system using inductive resonant coupling via custom copper spiral coils — applying RF near-field energy transfer principles to a practical embedded system.

  • Designing custom copper spiral coil arrays and optimizing coil geometry for maximum coupling efficiency and minimal EMI
  • Developing PCB layout to interface power electronics with the coil array
  • Integrating a status display with embedded firmware for power regulation and device detection
